# Cursed Workshop 0.12.12

Unofficial community tool for Call to Arms - Gates of Hell: Ostfront Dynamic Conquest.

## New

- Weapon attributes: a new tool to edit each weapon of the vehicle on its own (main gun, coaxial MG, hull MG...):
  - reload time, rate of fire, rounds per magazine and range, for the AI and the player alike;
  - pause between bursts, burst length and spread;
  - per ammunition type: muzzle velocity, penetration at each distance, and damage to armour and to infantry;
  - the total ammunition the vehicle carries.

  Only that vehicle changes. It gets its own copy of the weapon, so other vehicles and the AI keep the original.
- Play button at the top right: starts Gates of Hell through Steam.
- Creative does everything: a chassis from Creative opens the same design editor as one from your campaign. You can add several turrets, change guns, remove turrets, add fittings or convert it to a support vehicle.
- Choose where a build goes with two checkboxes: your campaign, the Conquest shop, or both. "Shop" uses no unit and leaves your campaign untouched.
- A Creative build can go into your campaign as a new unit. It uses none of your units, and its crew seats are empty so you fill them in game.

## Fixes

- Towed guns keep the crewmen their animations need. The 2 cm Flak 30's commander loads it, and the game crashed on direct control without him, so he can no longer be left behind. Guns built before get him back with "Update vehicles".
- No more crash when applying the mod list ("Block terminator expected" in a modular_workshop .inc file). On vehicles whose donor weapon had a timed effect with two numbers ({delay 2.5 0.5}), the second number was renamed as if it were a name. "Update vehicles" repairs vehicles built before, including shop-only ones.
- Clearer mod names in the game's mod list: the tool shows as "Cursed Workshop - Tool (DO NOT ACTIVATE)" and your vehicles as "Cursed Workshop - Built Vehicles (ACTIVATE THIS)". The new vehicles name appears after your next build or "Update vehicles".
- An older copy of Cursed Workshop no longer changes vehicles built by a newer one. It stops and asks you to use the latest version. Reopening an old version after updating could leave the game crashing when loading the mod.
- Towed guns (2 cm Flak 30 and others) count their real crew. The push and tow places are the same soldiers, not extra ones. Leaving a crewman behind also removes his push and tow places, so the rest of the squad can board. Use "Update vehicles" to fix guns built before.
- An edited weapon shows its real name in the game (e.g. "PaK 38 L/60 de 5 cm") instead of desc/weapon/mw_..._gun.
- Building with edited weapon attributes no longer stops with "Unknown state profile fields".
- Replace gun with "Keep the original mantlet" no longer leaves the old barrel in place when the barrel carries a part of its own (280 mm Br-5 and others). Only the cradle stays.
- "Barrel only" takes the real barrel. It used to take the mantlet on the StuG III, KV-2, SU-76, Grille and others, and the cradle on the 305 mm MB-2-12.
- A gun whose vehicle carries its ammunition without the shell type (305 mm MB-2-12) can be used as a donor.
- Trucks and cars whose door or hatch sounds take a parameter (Opel Blitz, CCKW, Ford, ZiS, GAZ and about 60 more) no longer stop the build with "Parameterized sound macro needs adapter: animation_sound".
- Replacing a gun with a chassis from Creative no longer says it needs a recipient from your campaign.
- Position, rotation and pivot fields show two decimals instead of numbers like -6.18245068359375, and have no spin arrows, so the number fits.

## Install or update

Download `CursedWorkshop-0.12.12-win64.zip`, extract the entire archive and run
`CursedWorkshop/CursedWorkshop.exe`. Keep the `_internal` folder beside the executable. Players do not need
Python or Node.js. The Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime is required for the desktop window; `--browser` uses
the default browser instead. See [Microsoft's WebView2 Runtime download](https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/).

Close the old editor before updating. Your built vehicles stay in the game's `mods/modular_workshop` folder
and your editor data in `%LOCALAPPDATA%/ModularWorkshop`. Keep campaign and editor-data backups.

The ZIP includes installation instructions, the user guide and third-party notices.
