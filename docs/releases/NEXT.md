Cursed Workshop (next version)

New
   * Design templates: save everything you did to a vehicle under a name (armour, commander MG, guns and turrets from other vehicles, mobility, turret and gun limits, crew, smoke, charges) and apply it to another vehicle of the same model. Pick that vehicle with Chassis or Edit, then use the new Templates button at the top. If the template uses parts from other vehicles, the tool picks vehicles of the same models from your campaign and spends them once when you build; if one is missing, it tells you which.
   * Favorites: every long list (armour, MGs, supports, bones, campaigns...) has a star next to each option. Star the ones you use and press Favorites next to the search box to see only those. Each list keeps its own favorites, even after closing the tool.
   * Chassis mobility: change the vehicle's top speed, reverse speed, pivot turn, speed in tight turns, power, track grip, fuel and range. The game works out normal and sprint speed, acceleration, turning and consumption from them, like on an original vehicle.
   * Casemate aiming: when the main gun can only turn a little, the whole vehicle now turns to aim, like a native tank destroyer.
   * Gun mantlets as extra armour: put another vehicle's mantlet on your gun. It goes on the gun's elevation, so it moves up and down with it, and comes without the breech that shares its model.
   * Armour list: sort by most useful, A to Z or thickness, and filter by nation. Protection type is now a checklist, so you can combine types (side skirts and plates, no accessories). Identical pieces appear once, and test or unusable pieces are hidden.
   * Shop: buy your custom vehicles in any saved campaign without researching their parts first. Set your own price (free too) or go back to the suggested one, and take a listing off the shop whenever you want; vehicles you already own stay. The AI doesn't recruit custom vehicles.
   * Passenger MGs: move and rotate the whole MG, its tripod, the gun and the operator separately, with the arrows or with numbers, even before placing it. Each mounted MG has its own slot, aim and sight, and commander MGs follow the same rules. Passenger seats have readable names in game.
   * Numbers are tucked away: the X/Y/Z, traverse and elevation fields of passenger MGs, passenger seats, pivot and position are inside a closed "Numeric values" section. Moving and turning with the arrows and rings comes first; open the section when you want exact values.
   * Faster tool: resources and catalogs load faster, previews reuse what was already read, and the 3D view only redraws while something changes.

Fixes
   * Vehicles built with a mortar or a towed gun no longer sink halfway into the ground for the whole battle (T-28, Sd.Kfz with mortar). The donor's ground contact points no longer come along with the gun.
   * The game no longer closes when loading the campaign with "Zero speed in ik-bone module_basis" (LVT(A)-1 with a 155 mm). No turret or gun turns slower than 0.5 degrees per second, the slowest the game accepts, and the limits tool doesn't let you set less.
   * The 3D view no longer shows vehicles and crew as their mirror image. Numbers and markings read the right way ("333" on the T-26), the Pz.Kpfw IV's driver and hull MG are on the correct side, and crewmen face the right way. Campaign photos are fixed too.
   * Weapon angles: armour, smoke launchers, passenger seats and towing or charge points fixed to a moving part now move with it. A mantlet on the gun goes up and down with the gun.
   * Most mantlets came out broken or with loose pieces. They are now taken by whole modelled pieces.
   * Extra armour pieces no longer appear on their side: a new piece starts upright, as it was on its own vehicle.
   * Turrets mounted on other turrets can still be selected after building, and keep their own position, limits and angles.
   * Multi-barrel weapons keep their own muzzle flashes, sounds and firing animations instead of firing from the centre or from another weapon.
   * Gun changes keep every barrel, the mantlet and the sights, including those of secondary weapons.
   * Some combined designs failed to build because of their sounds. Fixed.
   * Textures of moved parts are no longer lost.
   * Mirrored parts no longer show transparent barrels or inverted lighting.
   * Demolition charges keep their explosion.
   * Passenger MG fixes: editing the preview no longer sends the operator back to zero, seated crew no longer appear in inverted poses, and parts are no longer drawn twice.
   * Chassis mobility fields were squeezed letter by letter. They are now in two readable columns.
   * The design templates panel no longer takes half of the tool column: it opens from the Templates button at the top.
   * Diagnostics: downloading the .zip works again; the report also stays on disk.

Press "Update vehicles" once to apply these fixes to vehicles you already built, then restart the game before testing them.
