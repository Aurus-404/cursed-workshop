# Cursed Workshop 0.11.11

Unofficial community tool for Call to Arms - Gates of Hell: Ostfront Dynamic Conquest.

## New

- Made-to-measure armour: in Extra armor, "Made-to-measure plate" gives you a steel plate you size yourself. Set width, height and thickness in mm RHA, or stretch it with the arrows. The plate is as deep as it is thick, and you place it like any other piece. It comes with its own worn steel texture.
- Guns from the Depot: use a stored turret as the donor when you change a gun. It only offers the weapons on that turret, and building spends that part once, with no second unit from your campaign.
- "Turns with": in Position and mounting, choose whether an added turret stays fixed to the chassis or turns with another turret. It stays where you put it.
- Crew placement: move and rotate visible crew with the arrows, rings or numbers, and move their pivot. Moving a crewman no longer reloads the whole preview.
- Price before building: the build confirmation shows the suggested shop price and lets you type your own, in creative mode too.
- Shop clean-up: take test variants off the list and stop selling them in one step. You can bring them back; vehicles you already own stay.
- View > Armour (mm): the colour now covers the whole vehicle instead of leaving patches, and pointing at a plate shows a box next to the mouse with thickness, impact angle, effective thickness, RHA and material.
- New look: flatter, simpler interface with plain buttons, one accent colour, a slimmer top bar and even spacing. Each tool says in one line what it is for.
- Tool panels: Extra armor, Smoke launchers, Passenger MG, Passengers, Towing and Demolition charge are split into collapsible panels (choose a piece, place it, placed pieces).

## Fixes

- Changing a gun no longer fails to build when the donor has a part named like the new slot (LVT(A)-1 37 mm on a Cruiser Mk I hull MG).
- Multi-turret vehicles: swapping a turret no longer hides guns or coaxial MGs from the weapon bar.
- A donor's main gun can be picked even when its original mount was an MG mount.
- Vehicles with a main cannon list cannons before machine guns in the weapon bar.
- Single-barrel guns and MGs keep their muzzle flash after being moved to another slot, even after several changes.
- Added turrets take their own damage and can be repaired on their own, instead of knocking out the main turret. The tool warns you when the vehicle has no free slots left for that.
- Adding another turret no longer fails because of clashing part names.
- A turret that turns on its own on top of another turret no longer drags the lower one with it.
- The gun and its coaxial MG stay together when you move the gun's pivot.
- MGs no longer show up twice or out of place after a swap.
- B2 Flamm: replacing the flamethrower no longer fires twice or keeps the old weapon's projectiles.
- B2 740(f) and similar: other guns on the vehicle can be replaced again.
- Crew seats that belong to the donor's hull no longer end up under the vehicle or spin with the turret.
- Seated crew no longer appear mirrored in the preview.
- Crew fixed to a weapon mounted on a turret follows that turret.
- Main gun assemblies keep their own loaders and commanders.
- Crew of moved guns, anti-aircraft crews included, board and leave through doors that exist on the new chassis.
- Extra armour no longer jumps under the vehicle or disappears when Weapon angles is turned on.
- Reopening a built vehicle keeps its fittings editable without drawing a second copy under them.
- Editing or building after Update vehicles or after changing the Shop no longer fails.
- The tool uses much less memory while it prepares its lists after opening a campaign.
- Browser mode (Linux and Steam Deck): closing one tab no longer ends the tool while another is open, and a tab left behind tells you the tool has closed instead of showing network errors.
- The top bar is locked while a campaign loads.
- Turret and gun limit fields line up, and the right panel is no longer cut off at its edge.
- Diagnostics: reports include more of the vehicle's game files, load times and the crash log.

Press "Update vehicles" once to apply these fixes to vehicles you already built, then restart the game before testing them.

## Install or update

Download `CursedWorkshop-0.11.11-win64.zip`, extract the entire archive and run
`CursedWorkshop/CursedWorkshop.exe`. Keep the `_internal` folder beside the executable. Players do not need
Python or Node.js. The Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime is required for the desktop window; `--browser` uses
the default browser instead. See [Microsoft's WebView2 Runtime download](https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/).

Close the old editor before updating. Your built vehicles stay in the game's `mods/modular_workshop` folder
and your editor data in `%LOCALAPPDATA%/ModularWorkshop`. Keep campaign and editor-data backups.

The ZIP includes installation instructions, the user guide and third-party notices.
