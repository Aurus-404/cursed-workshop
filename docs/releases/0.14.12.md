# Cursed Workshop 0.14.12

Unofficial community tool for Call to Arms - Gates of Hell: Ostfront Dynamic Conquest.

## New

### More crew, and who works each weapon

- **Add extra crew.** The Crew tool has an **Add crew** button. Pick a loader, gunner, commander or driver and he goes on the part you are editing; place and remove him like a passenger seat. Each extra loader shortens his weapon's reload by the percentage you choose (15% by default, never below a quarter of the original). Extra gunners, commanders and drivers take over when someone falls. Each extra crewman is a real seat on the built vehicle and an extra slot in its campaign squad.
- **Choose who works each weapon.** The Crew tool lists every weapon with the crewman who aims and fires it, and you can give it to anyone aboard. One crewman can work several weapons, as on the game's own vehicles: the driver a hull MG, a loader a rear MG, the turret's gunner a rocket launcher on the turret.
- **Leave any crewman in reserve**, even one who works a weapon. His weapons pass to someone still aboard (the weapon's loader, then the main gunner, then whoever is left), so a Wanze launcher no longer needs its own soldier on the roof. Only the driver and the crew a towed gun needs stay locked.

### Machine guns and AT rifles anywhere

- **Weapons without a turret.** A new tool puts a weapon on the hull, the turret or any other part with no turret of its own, like the Sherman's bow MG. It gets its own traverse and elevation joints, which you place with Move/Rotate and limit in degrees, and its ammunition goes in the vehicle's box; pick who works it in Crew. By default the whole vehicle turns towards a target past its traverse, like a casemate; untick **Turn the vehicle at the limit** for a weapon that just waits.
- **An MG or AT rifle as the donor in Replace gun.** Under Donor gun, **Choose MG / AT rifle** lists the weapons a soldier can mount. Pick the gun to replace (a bow MG, a coaxial, even the main gun) and save: no second vehicle is needed. The gun becomes a slot the crew can swap in battle, like the Universal Carrier's Bren and Boys. **Position slot weapon** adjusts where it sits. Vehicles that already have such a slot (Universal Carrier, StuG III roof MG) can start with another weapon. The vehicle's own MG model stays drawn; take it off with Remove in game in the Pieces list.
- Both take machine guns (MG 42, MG 34, Bren, Browning, DP...) and anti-tank rifles (PzB 39, PTRD, Boys...). Towed guns such as a PaK and tripod weapons such as the Lafette are vehicles of their own in the game: they still go on with **Free: on the deck**.

### See what you are choosing

- **Lists mark what they name.** Point at an entry, or reach it with the keyboard, in any list that picks a part or a crewman (part selectors, joints, weapons, saved weapon changes, placed armor and accessories, "attach to" lists) and it lights up in the 3D view. A crewman with no visible seat gets a marker where he works.
- **Remove decoration in game.** In the Pieces list, **Remove in game** leaves a toolbox, spare track links or a stowage bin off the built vehicle and its wrecks; **Put back in game** brings it back. Only the look changes: the piece's armor, collisions and damage stay. Pieces of a donor turret can be removed once the turret change is applied.
- **A color for each kind of part** with the original textures off: chassis, turret, weapons, tracks and wheels, extra armor, accessories, crew and passengers, with a legend under the view. Change them in Settings → **Untextured view colors**, or go back to the old two colors.

### Simpler editor

- **One tool menu for the whole vehicle.** The Chassis and Turret tabs are gone. Tools sit in three categories that fold open or shut, and the editor remembers which are folded: Weapons and parts, Crew and passengers, Chassis and accessories. Position, Limits and Crew have their own part selector, and Turret and gun limits lists the turret's and the chassis' joints together.
- Shorter Crew and Replace gun panels, without the long help paragraphs. The recoverable draft notice looks like the other floating panels.

### Conquest shop

- **Edit shop vehicles.** Each vehicle in the Shop has an **Edit** button that opens its design with no campaign needed, using the catalog's parts as in Creative. Build and the shop sells the new version at the price you give it; the old custom price is kept by default. Units already bought in your campaigns keep working as they were; to change those, use Edit on them in the campaign. A vehicle made with mods that are not active opens once you enable them.

## Fixes

### Editor start, mods and campaigns

- **The editor opens again on PCs where it showed only "v" and no button worked.** Some installers make Windows list `.js` files as plain text, so the browser refused to run the interface. The editor now sets the types of its own files.
- **No more "define not found" crash** after changing mods (for example from MACE to Horten's Frontline). Every vehicle is registered again, and the mod check also catches vehicles that call an effect the current mods do not have.
- Opening a campaign warns when other mods load after **Cursed Workshop - Built Vehicles (ACTIVATE THIS)** and names them. A mod loaded after it can leave your vehicles without engine sound or unregistered.
- Recovering an autosaved draft no longer drops its armor, accessories, passenger MGs and seats, extra crew, commander MG or hidden crew.
- **Update vehicles** no longer skips designs whose turret brings its own commander, such as a Pz IV with a Pz.Sp.Wg. 204 turret ("Crew seat has no visible bone: gunner2"). The gunner's saved position no longer lands on that commander.
- Vehicles with a passenger MG operator seated like the Fi 156's rear gunner can be updated again ("Choose an available operator pose.").

### Crew and seats

- **Turret hatches close again.** When two seats had the same name, the editor renamed one "gunner2", which the game treats as an AA gunner standing in the hatch, so the hatch stayed open all battle (a Pz I with a Wanze launcher, a StuG III, an Sd.Kfz. 7/2...).
- **Towed guns on a carriage, such as the 2 cm Flak 30, no longer bring extra soldiers on foot.** The squad has as many soldiers as the gun has crew, and the editor shows that count.
- Moving a towed gun on the deck moves all its crew with it; some loaders stayed pinned to the hull's edge.
- A towed gun's loader shows again in designs that had him in reserve. The battle always put him aboard; the editor hid him.
- Crewmen left in reserve no longer reappear while you reopen a saved turret to adjust it.
- The 15.5 cm GPF K 418(f), and other units whose definition removes seats from a shared crew file, can be put on a vehicle again ("Donor crew place driver1 links missing bone driver1").

### Weapons and aiming

- **A gun added on the deck or as a second turret with little or no traverse** (a 155 mm GPF on a Char B2) turns the whole vehicle to aim, as the Char B2's own hull gun does. The vehicle's own side turrets keep the game's behavior.
- A weapon on a turret with no traverse of its own, such as the Wanze launcher, turns with the turret again instead of only elevating.
- Rocket launchers and mortars edited with the Weapons tool show their name in battle again instead of a code like "desc/stuff/mw_..._gun".
- Gun sights taken from another vehicle no longer jump behind the vehicle at full zoom (a Pz I with the Sd.Kfz. 251/17's 2 cm gun). The Vehicle sight tool gets the same fix.
- Guns whose ammunition is named without its nation, such as MACE's 37 mm trench gun, can be used again.
- Machine guns taken from a stand (DS-39, Maxim) aim on their own joints again and show their arcs.
- A commander MG or rider MG added to a vehicle with full stowage, such as a Sherman, no longer loses its ammunition: the inventory grows to fit it.

### Armor

- **Plates with a circular opening and hollow cylinders protect again.** In battle they read 0 mm and every round went through them; they are now built from solid pieces that fill the visible plate.
- Extra armor plates no longer show their paint with the original textures off.

### Editor view and layout

- In Turret and gun limits, Position and Crew, saved deck guns and other changed turrets light up again on hover. Mounts inside a replaced turret, such as the Char B2's original cupola MG, are no longer offered.
- A vehicle opened with Edit draws its turret in the turret color again in the plain view.
- Crewmen no longer have washed-out, blotchy trousers in the 3D view with textures on. Built vehicles were not affected.
- "Who works each weapon" shows only in the Crew tool.
- **Move pivot** sits in the same row as Move part and Rotate part. The Editor controls section in Settings is laid out like the others again.

## Install or update

Download `CursedWorkshop-0.14.12-win64.zip`, extract the entire archive and run
`CursedWorkshop/CursedWorkshop.exe`. Keep the `_internal` folder beside the executable. Players do not need
Python or Node.js. The Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime is required for the desktop window; `--browser` uses
the default browser instead. See [Microsoft's WebView2 Runtime download](https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/).

Close the old editor before updating. Your built vehicles stay in the game's `mods/modular_workshop` folder
and your editor data in `%LOCALAPPDATA%/ModularWorkshop`. Keep campaign and editor-data backups.
Use **Update vehicles** to apply these fixes to vehicles built before this release.

The ZIP includes installation instructions, the user guide and third-party notices.
